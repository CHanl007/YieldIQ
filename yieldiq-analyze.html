⚠️ Condo-Specific Risk Factors — Read Before Investing
  • Request the last 2 years of financial statements. Check the reserve fund balance — underfunded reserves are a serious red flag. Industry standard: reserves should cover at least 10% of the building's replacement cost.
  • Ask directly about pending special assessments. A single $15,000–$50,000 special assessment can wipe out years of projected cash flow. This is the most common surprise expense condo investors face.
  • Read the last 6–12 months of board meeting minutes. This is where the real story lives. Look for deferred maintenance discussions, litigation, insurance issues, and reserve shortfall debates.
  • Verify the master insurance policy. Florida condo insurance has become a crisis — some buildings are losing coverage entirely, making them unfinanceable. Confirm coverage is in place and what your individual policy must cover.
  • Review rental restrictions. Many condos limit the number of units that can be rented. Short-term rental (Airbnb) restrictions are common and can significantly impact your income strategy.
🚨 Florida SB 4D Alert: Buildings 3+ stories over 30 years old are subject to mandatory structural inspections and reserve funding requirements under Florida Statute 718. Many owners are currently receiving five and six-figure special assessments as a result. Verify the building's compliance status before proceeding.
